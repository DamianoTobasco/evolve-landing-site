Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Peptide Tracking is designed as a private tracking and education app. This policy explains what information may be collected and how it is used.

Information You Provide

The app may store information you choose to enter, including your name, onboarding answers, peptide names, schedules, dose logs, injection sites, notes, reminders, weight entries, progress photos, meal photos, body or face scan photos, check-ins, side effects, and settings.

HealthKit and Apple Fitness Data

If you grant permission, the app may read selected Apple Health or Apple Fitness data such as steps, workouts, active energy, weight, and related wellness metrics to display your progress. Health data is used only for app functionality. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with data brokers.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases and subscription status are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We may receive purchase status, product identifiers, entitlement status, and anonymous app user identifiers so premium access can be enabled.

Account and Sign-In

If you sign in with Apple, email, or another provider, the app may receive basic account information such as an identifier or email address. This is used to help restore your setup and premium access where supported.

Photos, Meal Analysis, and AI Features

If you use photo or AI-assisted features, images or text you submit may be processed to generate an app response, such as a meal estimate or educational summary. Do not upload sensitive images you do not want processed. AI outputs may be inaccurate and are not medical advice.

Local Storage

Most app data is stored on your device unless you enable a feature that requires syncing, account recovery, subscription validation, or AI/photo processing. Removing the app may delete local-only data unless it has been backed up by the operating system.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide reminders, logs, progress views, purchase access, customer support, app safety, analytics, feature improvement, and user-requested AI or scan features.

Deletion and Choices

You can delete account-related app data from Settings where available, revoke Health permissions in the iOS Health app, remove photos or logs inside the app, cancel subscriptions through Apple, or contact us for privacy questions.

Children

Peptide Tracking is not intended for children under 13. Do not use the app if you are not legally permitted to provide health-related information in your region.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to contact@theevolve.app.
