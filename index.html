      What is Evolve: Fitness Quest?

      An iPhone app that puts workouts, cardio, nutrition and meditation in one place, then turns what you log into XP, levels, five stats and a 40‑stage Journey. The idea is simple: your real‑life effort should be something you can see.

      Do I have to finish all three daily quests to keep my streak?

      No. One qualifying workout, meal log or meditation keeps your streak alive for the day. The other two quests are extra XP.

      What comes with Premium?

      Premium unlocks the AI features: photo food scanning, voice food logging and Fridge AI, which has a daily scan limit. It also includes the Hero Collection suits. Current pricing is on the App Store listing.

      How accurate are the calorie estimates?

      Photo and voice logs are AI estimates, not lab measurements. You see what was detected, adjust the portion, and choose the meal before anything gets logged.

      Does Evolve work with Apple Health?

      Yes. With your permission, Evolve can read available steps, distance, active calories, heart rate and workouts from Apple Health, and import supported workouts, including routes when they're available.

      Are the bosses actual fights?

      They're milestones. Each boss guards the last stage of a region on your Journey. Meet its real‑life requirements (workouts, streaks, meditation minutes, meals) and you claim the victory.
