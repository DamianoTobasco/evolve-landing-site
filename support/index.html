Account & sign-in

How do I get my progress on a new phone?

Install Evolve Arena and sign in the same way you did before: Sign in with Apple, Google or email. Your progress, including your Arena progress, is saved to your account.

I can't sign in.

Make sure you're using the same sign-in method as before. An account made with Sign in with Apple won't open with Google, and the other way round. Still stuck? Email us with the email address on your account.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your data, including your character, stats, achievements and workout history, and can't be undone. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription, so cancel Premium first (see below).

Premium & billing

What comes with Premium?

The Arena and all 10 heroes are free. Premium is an optional subscription that unlocks Premium hero skins, AI meal scanning, AI voice food logging and the "What's in my fridge?" scanner. Current prices are on the subscription screen and the App Store listing.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Evolve and tap Cancel Subscription. Premium stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple ID you paid with.

I paid, but Premium isn't showing.

Go to Profile → Settings → Plans & Subscription and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID you paid with. If it still doesn't show, email us and include your App Store receipt.

Arena & battles

Where do I find the Arena?

Tap the Arena banner on Home. Swipe or use the bottom bar to move between Journey, Cards, Battle, Chests and Store.

Do I have to work out to play?

No, you can battle straight away. Training makes your hero stronger: your real strength, cardio, mind, nutrition and consistency stats set your hero's damage, speed, reload and health.

My phone gets hot or the game stutters.

Open the Arena settings (the gear in the Battle lobby) and set Graphics quality to Battery saver. It applies from your next battle. A phone that gets hot also switches to Battery saver by itself mid-battle.

I lost a match. Did I lose my rank?

A loss can cost trophies, but it never drops you below a rank you've already reached.

Chests & rewards

How many chests can I get?

Up to 15 free chests a day, earned by playing. Chests you win wait for you until you open them.

Can I buy coins or chests?

No. Coins, chests and skins can't be bought with real money. Chests are free rewards from playing, and coins unlock skins in the Store. Premium hero skins come with Premium.

Where do I enter a creator code?

In the Arena, open the Store and use Support a creator. A creator code doesn't change any price or content; it just tells us who sent you.

Friends & online

How do I add friends?

Tap the friends button next to your hero in the Battle lobby, then share your friend code or invite link. When a friend accepts, the host picks a mode and map and you both ready up.

What do I get for inviting a friend?

When a friend joins with your invite link and finishes their first match, you both get a free gift chest.

Is there chat? Is it safe for kids?

There's no text or voice chat. Other players only see your hero name, the hero you play and preset emote pins. Hero names are checked against a blocklist, friends can only be added with a code or link, and you can remove a friend at any time from the Friends screen.

Tracking & Apple Health

How do I change what Evolve reads from Apple Health?

Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps under Privacy, and choose Evolve. You can turn each data type on or off there at any time.

How accurate are photo and voice calorie estimates?

They're AI estimates, not lab measurements. You see what was detected, adjust the portion and confirm before anything is logged.

What happens to my data?

Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why, and how to delete it.
